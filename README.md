## Overview

**Start Rise** is a habit tracker designed for people with ADHD, anxiety, depression, or anyone tired of all-or-nothing habit apps. Instead of punishing you for missed days, Start Rise celebrates partial progress and protects your momentum on hard days.

The app replaces traditional streaks with a flame-based momentum system that grows with every effort and dims gently instead of resetting to zero.

---

## Features

- **Momentum System**: Your flame grows with effort, not perfection. Partial completions count.
- **Spark Mode**: Low energy? Do the 2-minute version of any habit. It still counts.
- **Freeze Tokens**: Earn protection for hard days. Life happens—your progress shouldn't suffer.
- **Morning Energy Check**: Log how you're feeling and get adapted recommendations.
- **Beautiful Themes**: 12 carefully crafted themes from calming Lavender Dream to focused Paper mode.
- **ADHD-Friendly Limits**: Maximum 6 active habits to reduce overwhelm and increase focus.
- **Flexible Scheduling**: Daily, weekly, or specific days—habits that fit your life.
- **No Guilt Design**: No red "failure" colors, no shame—just gentle encouragement.

## Using Start Rise

### Creating Habits

- Tap the **+** button to create a new habit.
- Choose an icon (SF Symbol or emoji) and a gradient color.
- Set your schedule: daily, X times per week, or specific days.
- Add a **Spark Version**: a tiny 2-minute action for low-energy days.
- For timed habits, set a target duration.

### The Momentum System

Start Rise replaces rigid streaks with a momentum-based flame:

- **Full completion**: Flame grows by 1.0
- **Partial completion** (50%+): Flame grows by 0.5
- **Partial completion** (25%+): Flame grows by 0.25
- **Missed day**: Flame dims gently (not reset to zero)
- **Skipped with reason**: No penalty if you log low energy

Your flame level reflects consistent effort over time, not a fragile streak that breaks.

### Spark Mode

Every habit can have a "spark version"—a minimal 2-minute action:

- **Habit**: Exercise for 30 minutes → **Spark**: Do 5 jumping jacks
- **Habit**: Read for 20 minutes → **Spark**: Read one page
- **Habit**: Meditate → **Spark**: Take 3 deep breaths

On low-energy days, completing the spark version keeps your momentum alive.

### Freeze Tokens

Earn freeze tokens through consistency:

- Complete habits for 7 consecutive days to earn a token
- Maximum 3 tokens banked at once
- Use a token to protect your flame on sick days, rest days, or overwhelming days
- Tokens are automatically suggested when you mark a day as "low energy"

## Troubleshooting

- **Habits not appearing**:
  - Check if the habit is scheduled for today (frequency settings).
  - Ensure the habit is not archived (check Habits tab).

- **Flame level seems wrong**:
  - The flame reflects cumulative momentum, not just today's completions.
  - Check the habit detail view for full history.

- **App feels slow**:
  - Try reducing the number of active habits.
  - Restart the app to clear any temporary state.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Q: How many habits should I track?**

A: We recommend 3-6 habits maximum. Start Rise enforces a limit of 6 active habits by default to prevent overwhelm—a core ADHD-friendly principle.

**Q: What happens if I miss a day?**

A: Your flame dims slightly but doesn't reset. This is intentional—one bad day shouldn't erase weeks of progress. Use a freeze token if you're having a particularly hard day.

**Q: Can I track habits I want to stop (like smoking)?**

A: Yes! Create a "negative habit" to track days you successfully avoided something. The momentum system works the same way.

**Q: Is my data synced to the cloud?**

A: Currently, all data is stored locally on your device using SwiftData. Cloud sync may be added in a future update.

**Q: Is there a premium version?**

A: Some themes and features may be premium in the future, but core habit tracking functionality is free.
