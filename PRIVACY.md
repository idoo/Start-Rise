# Start Rise Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** 2026-02-01  
**Developer:** Ivan Verevkin  
**Contact:** ivan@0x.se

Start Rise is an ADHD-friendly habit tracking app designed to help you build momentum without guilt. This Privacy Policy explains what data the app accesses, how it is used, and the choices you have.

## Information We Collect

### Data Stored Locally
- **Habit Data:** Habit names, icons, schedules, completion logs, flame levels, and streak information are stored locally on your device.
- **User Preferences:** Theme selection, notification settings, energy logs, and app configuration are stored locally.
- **No Account Required:** Start Rise does not require account creation, login, or any personal identifiers to function.

### Data We Do NOT Collect
- **No Personal Identifiers:** We do not collect names, emails, phone numbers, or location data.
- **No Analytics:** We do not use third-party analytics services or track your usage patterns.
- **No Advertising:** We do not display ads or share data with advertisers.
- **No Server Transmission:** Your habit data never leaves your device.

## How We Use Your Information

- **Core Functionality:** All habit data is used solely to provide the app's features—tracking habits, calculating momentum, displaying progress, and sending optional reminders.
- **Local Processing:** All calculations, streaks, and recommendations happen on your device.
- **Notifications:** If you enable reminders, iOS handles notification delivery locally. No notification content is sent to external servers.

## Data Storage and Security

- **Local Storage:** All data is stored using Apple's SwiftData framework in your device's secure app container.
- **No Cloud Sync:** Currently, Start Rise does not sync data to any cloud service. Your data exists only on your device.
- **Backup:** Your Start Rise data may be included in iCloud or iTunes backups if you have device backup enabled. This is controlled by your iOS settings, not by Start Rise.
- **Encryption:** Data at rest is protected by iOS device encryption when you have a passcode enabled.

## Third Parties

- **No Third-Party SDKs:** Start Rise does not include third-party analytics, crash reporting, or advertising SDKs.
- **No Data Sharing:** We do not sell, rent, or share your data with any third parties.
- **Future Features:** If cloud sync or other features that involve data transmission are added in the future, they will be opt-in and this policy will be updated accordingly.

## Your Choices

- **Fully Offline:** Start Rise works entirely offline. No internet connection is required.
- **Notifications:** You can enable or disable habit reminders in the app settings or iOS Settings.
- **Data Deletion:** Deleting the app removes all Start Rise data from your device. You can also reset your data from within the app settings.
- **Export:** Future versions may include data export functionality to give you full control over your information.

## Children's Privacy

Start Rise is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Since the app collects no personal information from any user, this is inherently enforced.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app changes or new features are added. Material changes that affect data handling will be noted with an updated effective date. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

## Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights regarding your personal data. Since Start Rise:
- Stores all data locally on your device
- Does not transmit data to external servers
- Does not collect personal identifiers

You have full control over your data through your device. Delete the app to remove all data.
